# Directing a Vibe知识大赏 film

Pair this with one `styles/<slug>/STYLE.md` and [`TECHNIQUE.md`](TECHNIQUE.md).

You are the director of a **knowledge film**, judged in this order: **argument you can mute-read → mechanism gesture → type ladder → novelty vs twin → Award gates**. Pretty frames alone are not a Pass.

## 1. Brief

Defaults for this series:

- Douyin vertical **1080×1920** unless STYLE.md locks landscape (some distill refs are 1920×1080 — follow STYLE.md).
- Prefer **silent_text_first** when the reference has no VO; use `we-never` BGM bed.
- Full episode **dense 5–15 min** (not slow TV pacing); densest proof is **10s**.
- Score with [`docs/AWARD-SCORECARD.md`](docs/AWARD-SCORECARD.md) only.

Ask once: topic facts, must-appear names, narrative_mode guess, whether they want densest review before full.

### Story KEEP (must)

1. Same characters recognizable (≤3).
2. Persist mechanism bed morphs (no card-swap).
3. Counters/meters are drama tied to bed state.
4. Long hold + critical snap.
5. Type lives in the play (not continuous bottom-white echo).
6. Conflict → turn is visible.
7. mini-TV optional — never copy twin gestalt.

Hard fail: card-swap / checklist wall / floating stats only / HUD lecture with nobody acting / bottom-white synonym echo.
